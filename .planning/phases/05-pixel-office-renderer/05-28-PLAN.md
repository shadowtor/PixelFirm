<objective>
Close UAT gap G-05-4 (High: the handoff line starts at x = 0 and overlaps other agents' glyphs; the connection must originate at the sender and terminate at the receiver) and the visual half of G-05-1b (High: the black banner dominates the office). 05-29 finishes G-05-1b with shorter templates and the UI-SPEC amendment.

Root causes (.planning/debug/handoff-line-clamp.md, handoff-banner-size.md): `resolveDialogueBox` sizes an opaque strip to a full sentence in an 11 px font (210-283 px of a 320 px room), stacks it above the glyph slot (y = -6 clamped to 0 on the first desk row, over every glyph there), centres then clamps it into [0, 320 - w] (pinned at x = 0 for left-side speakers), and draws no tail — nothing ties it to either participant.

User decision (UAT test 4, authoritative): fix the line positioning — not move desks — so the connection originates from the sender and terminates at the receiver only.

Design, and why it does not ride on desk placement:
- The rule itself is layout-free: the bubble hangs from the speaker's foot line, spans the speaker and its partner, and is clamped only to the floor interior. Nothing in it reads desk rows.
- Whether that band is free of other agents is a property of the layout data, so it is checked FROM the layout data: a guard test iterates the rows `SEATS` and `STANDING_SPOTS` use, places characters at every other layout position through the real renderer, and asserts the band is clear. 05-27 guarantees speakers stand only on seat rows (its interaction tile never leaves the receiver's seat row). Any later layout edit that puts an agent or glyph into a speaker band fails this test instead of silently producing an overlap.
- 05-21 renders at device resolution, so the text can shrink to a 5 world-px font that is still 15-30 device px on screen.

D-04 (dialogue shown during the sequence) stays met; HANDOFF-02's determinism is untouched (templates are not edited here).

Output: partner-aware speech bubble under the pair's feet, the layout guard, the 16+4-position sweep, and live TRUTH 5.
</objective>
