<objective>
Close 05-VERIFICATION.md **gap 1**: the handoff dialogue is generated, stored in `Character.bubbleText`, and never painted. `renderScene` has no text pass, so ROADMAP criterion 3 and 05-CONTEXT.md **D-04** ("Dialogue/text shown during the sequence remains deterministic/template-based per HANDOFF-02") are unmet on the dialogue half. The `TaskState.title` length cap lands WITH the draw pass, not after it, as the verifier required.

Also closes the latent half of review CR-01 that the verifier kept as advisory (see `<planner_assumptions>` A5): the choreography FSM is the only non-idempotent consumer of relayed events, so it gets an event-id guard.

**The geometry, worked out, so the executor does not re-derive it.** Canvas 320x176 at zoom 1, offset 0. A character on interior row r draws its 16x32 sprite from `drawY = 16r - 24` (IDLE) or `16r - 18` (TYPE, +6 sitting offset). `resolveBubbleY(drawY, 13, 1)` puts the 13-row glyph slot at `drawY - 15`. Desk rows are 3/6/9 (05-10), so each desk-row band is fully used by sprite + glyph + gap, with 1px to spare — **there is no position for an 11px line that overlaps nothing**. The chosen rule, and what it costs:

- The dialogue box sits directly above the owner's glyph slot (`glyphSlotY - BUBBLE_ICON_GAP_PX - boxHeight`), clamped at y = 0; horizontally centred on the owner and clamped into the canvas.
- Row-3 owners (every agent in an office of up to 18): box lands at y 0..12, on the top wall strip, and overlaps the top 4 rows of the glyph slot. Row-6 owner: box y 42..54, above its glyph slot (57), over the lower half of the row-3 desk row in front. Row 9: y 90..102.
- The box never overlaps its OWNER's sprite (box bottom 13 <= 24 for row 3; 55 <= 72 for row 6).
- Because dialogue can overlap glyph rows, the render order becomes **sprites -> dialogue -> glyphs**: a state glyph (OFFICE-03) is never hidden by transient dialogue. This moves glyphs out of each character's z-sorted drawable into a final pass — strictly stronger than 05-07's "a glyph can never sort behind a character in front of it", because a glyph now sorts behind nothing at all.

Purpose: make criterion 3's dialogue visible, owner-bound, bounded in length, and unable to hide a state signal.
Output: a capped template function, a three-pass renderer with an owner-bound dialogue box, an FSM that clears its dialogue at sequence end and cannot be re-driven by a re-delivered event, and tests that assert the text's arrival in draw calls rather than the string's content alone. Real-canvas pixel evidence for the same path is 05-16's job.
</objective>
