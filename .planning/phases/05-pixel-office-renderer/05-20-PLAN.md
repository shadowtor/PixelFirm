<objective>
Close the one open gap in 05-VERIFICATION.md (truth 11, review CR-01): a handoff permanently deletes the sender's real status glyph. The arrival overwrites any glyph with the handoff-task icon, and the completion and the retire set it to null. The reducer never changes the sender's status on a handoff, so no status update comes along to repair it. A TESTING sender comes home typing with no glyph, which reads as CODING. A BLOCKED or WAITING_FOR_CEO sender comes home frozen with no glyph, which loses OFFICE-03's signal.

The fix mirrors 05-19's `restPose`: store the glyph, derive the display.
- `Character.statusBubble` holds the status glyph. Only the status upsert writes it.
- One writer, `applyBubble`, derives the displayed `bubbleType` from it at all four sites (upsert, arrival, completion, retire), so the glyph comes back when the handoff ends.
- Precedence, decided explicitly as the verifier asked: a frozen glyph always wins (D-03, OFFICE-03), the task icon wins over other glyphs while the sender waits at the receiver (D-04, HANDOFF-01), and otherwise the status glyph shows. See planner assumption A1 for why this plan does not use the review's "status glyph always wins".

It also folds in advisory WR-01, as the verifier asked. The sender-identity rule from 05-19 (WR-03) is applied in every phase, ICON_VISIBLE included, and in the completion handler and `retireHandoff`. A sender that vanishes while waiting at the receiver no longer leaves a live record, and a re-seated sender's completion no longer flashes an accepted line for one frame.

Purpose: OFFICE-01, OFFICE-03 and HANDOFF-01 are held at Gaps Found only by this defect. D-03 (frozen glyph plus frame hold) and D-04 (the walk, task icon, accept and return sequence tied to the real event pair) both hold on every path afterwards. D-01, D-02 and D-05 are untouched (05-02, 05-06 and 05-12 satisfy them).

Output: `Character.statusBubble`, `applyBubble`, `senderIsCurrent`, seven new real-loop tests in two nested describes, and no change to any existing test.

Out of scope: review IN-01 (stale comments) through IN-06, the harness warnings WR-02 through WR-05, and the claude-adapter items carried from earlier rounds. The verifier classed them as advisory, and none of them makes the office show a wrong state on today's paths.
</objective>
