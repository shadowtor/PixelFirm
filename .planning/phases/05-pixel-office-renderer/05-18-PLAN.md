<objective>
Close 05-VERIFICATION.md gap 2 (05-REVIEW.md CR-01). `runQuery` takes its ownership token only after `await attemptGracefulStop(record)`. When two calls wait on the same preemption, both take over the record one after the other, leaving an un-aborted `query()` that pause/cancel can no longer reach. A pause or cancel that lands during that wait is undone the same way.

Purpose: the race is latent today (no in-repo caller of sendMessage/resumeTask/pauseTask/cancelTask), but it becomes reachable as soon as Phase 6 wires one. Pause and cancel are the human's only stop controls over an agent acting in a real repository.

Output: the token is claimed before the await and re-checked after it. Pause and cancel claim a fresh token. Two tests fail on today's code and pass after.
