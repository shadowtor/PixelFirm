Close UAT gaps G-05-4 (High: the handoff line starts at x = 0 and overlaps other agents' glyphs; the connection must originate at the sender and terminate at the receiver) and G-05-1b (High: the "Handing off..." black banner dominates the office and reads as a debug banner).

Root causes (.planning/debug/handoff-line-clamp.md, handoff-banner-size.md): `resolveDialogueBox` sizes an opaque strip to a full sentence in an 11 px font (210-283 px of a 320 px room), stacks it above the glyph slot (y = -6 clamped to 0 on the first desk row, over every glyph there), centres then clamps it into [0, 320 - w] (pinned at x = 0 for left-side speakers), and draws no tail — nothing ties it to either participant.

User decision (UAT test 4, authoritative): fix the line positioning — not move desks — so the connection originates from the sender and terminates at the receiver only. Planner design within that decision:
- 05-24's layout keeps the band directly under a seated pair's feet (their own desk row) free of agents and glyphs, and 05-26 puts the sender on the tile beside the receiver, so the bubble goes there: tail up to the speaker's feet, box spanning the pair.
- 05-21 renders at device resolution, so the text can shrink to a 5 world-px font that is still 15-30 device px on screen.
- Templates shrink to a label ("<title> → <receiver>"), amending the 05-UI-SPEC copy the gap says must change. D-04's requirement that dialogue is shown during the sequence stays met; HANDOFF-02's determinism rule is untouched.

Output: partner-aware owner-anchored speech bubble, short templates, rebased tests, UI-SPEC typography/copy, live TRUTH 5.
