<objective>
Close UAT gap G-05-1d (Medium): the sender appears hidden behind the receiver. Root cause (.planning/debug/handoff-sender-overlap.md): the FSM walks the sender to the receiver's own seat tile with an empty blocked set, so the sender snaps onto the identical (x, y), ties on zY and is painted under the receiver (22% of its pixels visible); the same empty set lets paths cross other agents.

Also closes G-05-1e's "real blocked tiles for findPath" item: every walk now avoids furniture (from 05-24's layout) and other agents.

Fix: a defined interaction tile (the nearest free tile on the receiver's seat row, searched outward from its seat), an occupancy-aware blocked set for every walk, and the sender facing the receiver on arrival — per D-04 (walk over, task icon, accept, walk back) with the whole sequence still driven only by the real event pair. The pod layout from 05-24 always leaves a free neighbour on the seat row: the tile between a pod's two seats, or the aisle next to it. Searching from the receiver's SEAT (not its current tile) and only along its seat row keeps every handoff speaker on a seat row, even when the receiver is itself mid-walk or three senders arrive at once — 05-28's bubble placement is guarded on exactly that property.

Output: `interactionTileFor`, `blockedTilesFor`, a read-only `getCharacters()` accessor, rebased and new real-loop tests, and a live sender-visibility check.
</objective>
