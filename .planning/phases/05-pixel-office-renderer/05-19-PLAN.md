<objective>
Close the one open gap in 05-VERIFICATION.md: the 05-17 prohibition and the phase goal. A handoff must never strand a character in TYPE on another agent's desk or frozen mid-path, and must never drop a real completion. The verifier reproduced three paths through the real `stepOffice` loop (review CR-01). All three share one root cause: "path is empty" is used to mean "not walking". That misses the last frame of a walk (path empty, state still WALK) and a walk that never starts because the character already stands on the target tile. On top of that, the completion handler writes TYPE onto the receiver with no walk guard at all.

The fix makes WALK the single source of truth for "walking":
- Arrival is "not walking and nothing left to walk".
- Every pose write goes through one writer that never interrupts WALK. The writer remembers the pose and the walk ends in it, which also closes review IN-03: a CODING sender no longer shows IDLE after every handoff.
- `walkCharacterTo` called for the tile the character already stands on drops any pending path rather than no-opping.

The same "one character, one walk" invariant also closes WR-01 (retire records by sender, not only by taskId), WR-03 (a re-seated sender is a new character, compared by identity) and WR-02 (the handoff icon survives a glyph-less status). The verifier asked for all three to be folded into this plan.

Purpose: HANDOFF-01 is the only open requirement in Phase 5, and this gap is the only thing holding it at Gaps Found. D-04's walk, icon, accept and return sequence must stay tied to the real event pair on every path, not just the nominal one. D-03's frozen-frame rule is unchanged. D-01, D-02 and D-05 are untouched (05-02, 05-06 and 05-12 satisfy them).

Output: `Character.restPose` and `setRestPose`; an at-target `walkCharacterTo`; a `hasArrived` predicate; `HandoffRecord.fromChar`; retire-by-sender; `isWaitingHandoffSender`; seven new real-loop tests (paths a, b, b2, c, WR-01, WR-02, WR-03); and one flipped assertion in 05-17 test a1.

Out of scope, deferred: review WR-05 (the claude-adapter graceful stop is unbounded because `interrupt()` is awaited before the 5 s race) and IN-01 (which goes with it). Both are latent: nothing in production calls the runtime yet. They must land before Phase 6 wires a caller, and this plan does not touch `packages/claude-adapter`. IN-04 (a sprite jumps back up to one tile when re-routed mid-step) is cosmetic and was not requested.
</objective>
