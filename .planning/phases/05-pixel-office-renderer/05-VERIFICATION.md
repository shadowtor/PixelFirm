The sixth gap, G-05-P6, is where the round overreached. Removing the footer's `rgba(0,0,0,0.6)` backdrop did eliminate the black strip the CEO objected to — and the full-viewport sizing, centring and WALL_COLOR surround all work, with 0 near-black pixels at both documented OBS sizes. But the backdrop was also the only thing making the 11px `#cccccc` attribution legible independently of what sat behind it. `MIN_DISPLAY_SCALE = 3` guarantees a 960x528 canvas, and the footer is `position: fixed`, so on any viewport smaller than that the canvas scrolls under the footer and the licence notice lands on the floor planks at 4.40:1 and 3.21:1 — below WCAG AA. The fix is one property and one stronger assertion: `background: WALL_COLOR` on the footer — the same value the surround already uses, so G-05-P6's no-black-frame property is completely untouched and the contrast becomes unconditional at 6.74:1 — plus an equality assertion on that declaration and one below-`MIN_DISPLAY_SCALE` viewport in the harness so the overflow case is covered at all.
