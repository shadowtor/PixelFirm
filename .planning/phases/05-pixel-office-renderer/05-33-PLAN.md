<objective>
Close UAT gap G-05-P1 (polish item 1): speech bubbles must choose a safe position, never crossing the viewport edge and not covering desks when a clear spot exists.

Root cause (05-UAT.md): `resolveDialogueBox` has a single candidate. It always hangs under the speaker's feet, centred on the pair and clamped only into the floor interior, which on seat rows 4/8 lands on the desk and monitors every time.

Fix, which is also the planner's decision: an ordered candidate list scored against real per-frame obstacles. Glyphs and the floor bounds are hard limits. After that come desks, then all other furniture, then characters, then candidate order. This follows the UAT's own ranking ("first in-bounds candidate that covers no desk, else the least desk overlap"). The rule is layout-independent: it reads the frame's actual characters, glyphs and furniture, and it deliberately does not depend on where handoff senders stand. 05-34 moves senders to fixed aisle slots (G-05-P2), so these properties must hold for any speaker position. Worked through by hand on the shipped layout:
- a seat-row speaker gets "above" (rows 1-3 are clear);
- a row-4 accepted line gets "above";
- a row-6 speaker gets "below" when row 8 is empty under it;
- when row 8 is full, a row-6 speaker gets whichever of "below"/"above" covers no desk, even if that crosses seated agents' shoulders, rather than the desk-level "beside".
Desk-before-character is the user's ranking. A bubble is transient and sits under the glyph pass, so no stuck-state signal is ever hidden.

Output: the candidate placement and tail, scorer unit tests, position-agnostic scene sweeps, live TRUTH 5 rebased on the new properties, and the UI-SPEC placement rule.
</objective>
