Desks in front of the seats (rows 5 and 9) leave the band just under each seated pair's feet free of agents and glyphs — 05-27 anchors the handoff label there. A two-row top wall was rejected because it cannot fit two pod rows with that headroom.

Decisions honoured: D-01 (each status keeps its pose and animation — typing frames are never swapped for standing ones), D-03 (glyph overlay untouched), D-05 (only 05-22's audited MetroCity sprites are wired).
