**Honest framing of gap 3.** The churn path is currently latent in production: `deriveAgentStatus` never returns `OFFLINE` and no reducer handler sets it (05-03's documented gap), so nothing in the live pipeline despawns a character today. The defect is real in the public contract — `upsertCharacterFromAgent(id, OFFLINE)` despawns without freeing the desk — and it becomes live the day an agent-offline producer lands. It is fixed now because the fix is smaller than the explanation. The tests drive the public function directly, which is the only path that reaches it.

**Mechanism.** Desks are derived from state instead of from a counter: the next desk is the lowest-numbered slot whose tile no seated character holds. No free list, no bookkeeping to reset, and on an empty floor it reproduces the old order exactly. Identity hue keeps 05-10's FNV-1a bucket as the preference, then probes forward to the first bucket no seated character holds — collisions start at the thirteenth concurrently seated agent, which is what the record always claimed and was not true.

Purpose: the office never shows one figure where the company has two, and two seated agents are tellable apart up to twelve.
